- Vault: a new Bookmarks section keeps your own saved links, for example onion addresses, next to Passwords, Notes, Gallery, Documents and Wallet. A bookmark has a title, a link that must start with http:// or https:// (any host, .onion included) and optional notes; the list shows the title and the host, searches both, and a tap asks the usual question before the link opens in the browser. Long-press or the menu of a bookmark offers Open link, Edit, Copy link and Delete. A title that consists only of formatting or invisible characters is refused, and such characters are not shown in a title. Bookmarks are stored as password items with a kind marker, so a vault export, a password change and an older version of the app keep them: an older version lists them under Passwords with an empty password.
