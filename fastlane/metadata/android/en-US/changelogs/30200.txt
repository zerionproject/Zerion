v3.0.2: reliability, I2P and translations

A broad stability release. Remote contact adding over links is fixed: an upgraded account could sit on "connecting" because its post-quantum handshake key was regenerated on each start instead of stored once. If a contact is still stuck, remove it and add it again with a fresh link. The calculator decoy no longer freezes while you enter the code, and I2P now connects and reconnects reliably, with an optional off-by-default direct bootstrap for places where Tor is blocked.

Dozens of smaller fixes across messaging, backups, groups and channels: signing in works correctly when profiles share a password, backup import shows clear error messages, documents open from the media gallery, and several stuck or dead-end screens now behave properly. The network status screen reflects real connectivity.

Twelve languages are now fully translated: Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Arabic and Dutch.

No database upgrade from 3.0.1.

Full notes: https://github.com/zerionproject/Zerion/releases/tag/v3.0.2
