Changelog bullets for the 3.0.19 section (teams and organisations):

- Groups for teams: a group no longer needs every pair of members to be contacts; one person creates it, shares one permanent invite link, accepts the requests and invites everyone, and the Group owner's phone passes posts on between members who are not each other's contacts whenever it is online. The group settings screen now has a "Set up a team" help page that explains this and the limits (up to 256 members, 200 contacts with a private connection per phone, the owner's phone should be online often), the "Who your posts reach" dialog has an "Add contact" button, and [docs/TEAM_SETUP.md](docs/TEAM_SETUP.md) is the guide for administrators, including what happens while the owner is offline: posts wait on the sender's phone and the owner passes them on when back, with nothing stored on any server.
- Private connections: a phone keeps up to 200 contacts with a private connection (Tor client authorization). The 201st contact that asks for one is now kept on the open address and its connection details say so, while the 200 keep their private connection. Before, the private service was silently left unpublished for every contact once a 201st key arrived, so the private connections of all 200 could stop working without a word. Nothing changes for phones with fewer than 200 contacts.
